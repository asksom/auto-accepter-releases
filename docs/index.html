Never miss a queue pop.
Walk into champ select ready.

Auto Accepter is a small, free Windows app that accepts your League of Legends queue and sets up champion select for you: picks with backups for every role, your ban, summoner spells and rune pages.

Download for Windows
Free · Windows 10 and 11 · Updates itself

What it does

Accepts the queue

The match is accepted the moment it pops, so you can grab a drink while you wait.

Picks for every role

Set a pick and two backups for Top, Jungle, Mid, Bot and Support. If your champion is banned or taken, the next one is used.

Bans

Your ban is hovered and locked on your turn, and it skips any champion a teammate is hovering.

Summoner spells

Flash and Smite for jungle, Teleport for top: spells are set per role automatically.

Rune pages

Save a rune page for a champion once. When you lock that champion in, the page is set for you.

Your choice of lock-in

Lock in right away, wait until the last seconds, or only hover and lock in yourself.

Get started

  1. Download and run the installer. Windows may say it protected your PC because the app isn't signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

  2. Open the League client and log in. The bar at the top turns green when the app is connected.

  3. Choose your champions, ban and spells. Auto Accept is on from the start, and your setup is remembered next time.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, completely free.

How does it get updates?

The installed app checks for new versions on start and every few hours, downloads them in the background and asks you to restart.

Does it work on Mac?

No, it's Windows only for now.

Where are my settings stored?

Only on your PC. The app talks to the League client on your own computer and doesn't send your settings anywhere.

Is there a version without an installer?

Yes, download the portable zip, unzip it and run Auto Accepter.exe. The portable version doesn't update itself.
